Xiaofeng Li, **Leyi Sheng**, Zhen Sun, Zongmin Zhang, Jiaheng Wei, and Xinlei He.

IP-Bench is the first systematic benchmark designed to evaluate image protection methods in image-to-video generation scenarios. It evaluates representative protection methods and state-of-the-art I2V models, including robustness under preprocessing attacks and cross-model and cross-modality transferability.
