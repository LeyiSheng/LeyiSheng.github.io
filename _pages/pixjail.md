**Leyi Sheng**, Han Sun, Zhen Sun, Yuntao Yue, Jinlin Wu, Xinlei He, and Jiaheng Wei.

PixJail is a self-evolving paper-to-pipeline agent framework for reproducible text-to-image jailbreak evaluation. It constructs paper-specific attack modules and runnable evaluation pipelines under a unified contract while maintaining reusable, versioned reproduction knowledge.
