  1. PixJail: Self-Evolving Paper-to-Pipeline Reproduction for Text-to-Image Jailbreak Evaluation
    Leyi Sheng, Han Sun, Zhen Sun, Yuntao Yue, Jinlin Wu, Xinlei He, and Jiaheng Wei
    arXiv preprint, Jun 2026
    Abs arXiv Bib HTML

    As text-to-image jailbreak techniques evolve rapidly, existing benchmarks and reproduction workflows often struggle to keep pace. PixJail is a self-evolving paper-to-pipeline agent framework for reproducible text-to-image jailbreak evaluation. It constructs paper-specific attack modules and runnable evaluation pipelines under a unified contract while maintaining reusable, versioned reproduction knowledge.

  2. Stego Battlefield: Evaluating Image Steganography Attacks and Steganalysis Defenses
    Zhen Sun, Zongmin Zhang, Leyi Sheng, Yule Liu, Yifan Liao, Ke Li, Xinhu Zheng, Jiaheng Wei, Wenyuan Yang, and Xinlei He
    arXiv preprint, May 2026
    Abs arXiv Bib HTML

    SADBench systematically evaluates image steganography attacks and steganalysis defenses under practical security scenarios. It covers attack capability, defense capability, efficiency, and transferability across image-payload and text-payload steganography.

  3. IP-Bench: Benchmark for Image Protection Methods in Image-to-Video Generation Scenarios
    Xiaofeng Li, Leyi Sheng, Zhen Sun, Zongmin Zhang, Jiaheng Wei, and Xinlei He
    arXiv preprint, Apr 2026
    Abs arXiv Bib HTML

    IP-Bench is a systematic benchmark for evaluating image protection methods in image-to-video generation scenarios. It evaluates representative protection methods and state-of-the-art image-to-video models, including robustness, transferability, and practical preprocessing attacks.
